
# Initial Business Hypotheses

## 1. Admissions Automation

### Problem Hypothesis
Educational institutions receive enquiries from
students through websites, phone calls, WhatsApp
and other channels.

Managing these enquiries manually can result in
delayed responses, missed follow-ups and lost
admission opportunities.

### Proposed Solution
An AI-powered admissions assistant that can:

- Capture enquiries from multiple channels.
- Answer common admission-related questions.
- Qualify prospective students.
- Follow up with interested students.
- Track application progress.
- Notify admission counsellors about important leads.

### Target Customers
- Engineering colleges
- Universities
- Private educational institutions
- Coaching institutes

### Business Hypothesis
Institutions may pay a monthly subscription if
the platform reduces counsellor workload and
increases successful student conversions.

### Validation Questions
1. How many enquiries does an institution receive monthly?
2. How are enquiries currently managed?
3. How many leads are lost due to missed follow-ups?
4. How much time do counsellors spend on repetitive work?
5. Would institutions pay for an automated solution?

## 2. Real Estate Automation

### Problem Hypothesis
Real estate agencies and property developers receive
many property enquiries through websites, property
portals, phone calls and WhatsApp.

Sales teams spend significant time qualifying leads,
answering repetitive questions and scheduling visits.

### Proposed Solution
An AI-powered real estate assistant that can:

- Capture property enquiries.
- Understand customer requirements.
- Qualify buyers and tenants.
- Recommend matching properties.
- Schedule property visits.
- Automate customer follow-ups.
- Update the sales team's CRM.

### Target Customers
- Real estate agencies
- Property developers
- Real estate brokers
- Rental property management companies

### Business Hypothesis
Real estate businesses may pay a monthly subscription
if automation improves lead response time and helps
convert more enquiries into property visits and sales.

### Validation Questions
1. How many enquiries does an agency receive monthly?
2. How are leads currently tracked?
3. How much time is spent on repetitive follow-ups?
4. How many enquiries become actual property visits?
5. Would agencies pay for automated lead management?

---

## Initial Assumptions and Risks

- Businesses may already use CRM software.
- Existing WhatsApp and CRM integrations may be necessary.
- AI-generated answers must be accurate.
- Customer data must be handled securely.
- Willingness to pay has not yet been validated.

Interview at least five potential customers in each
